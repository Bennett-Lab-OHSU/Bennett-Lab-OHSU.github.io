{%- comment -%} The artwork shared by every red seal on the site. Included once in the page layout; each seal then reuses it and only adds its own two letters (see seal.html). Style: a solid, thick red border around a cream panel, like the heavy frames on imperial seals. The edges are deliberately uneven, as if pressed by hand. To make the edge calmer, lower the two "scale" numbers in the first filter (4.6 and 1.3). To make it wilder, raise them. Colors are #A63A2C (red) and #F6ECE0 (cream); the letters' red is set in seal.html. {%- endcomment -%}
