Put people's headshots in this folder.

Name each photo after the person's file in _members, plus .jpg (or .jpeg, .png, .webp).
For _members/jane-smith.md, upload jane-smith.jpg. It then appears on the People page
to the right of that person's stamp and description. No other change is needed.

Tips: crop to a portrait rectangle (4 wide by 5 tall), about 600 x 750 pixels,
under 300 KB. Remove location data from the photo before uploading.
